Features

Everything the service desk does, in one place

Built with a working dealership, around how a workshop actually runs from the moment a customer walks in to the moment they drive out.

🔎

Instant customer lookup

Search by phone, engine number, chassis number, registration or name, and see the full vehicle and service history.

🧾

Digital job cards

Showroom and home-service cards with their own numbering, mechanic assignment, complaints, hours and a printable slip.

🔩

Parts at the right price

Your full parts price list built in. Never below MRP, optional markup, warranty items marked free, oils and lubes tracked separately.

🔔

Service reminders

Next service worked out from hours and model. Send the reminder by SMS or WhatsApp with one tap, and see who has been reminded.

📊

Reports owners love

Daily and monthly numbers, income, mechanic work, and your brand's monthly workshop report, downloaded as Excel.

🛡️

Control & trust

Separate owner and staff logins, closed cards locked, and every edit to customers, prices or closed cards recorded.

How it works

Up and running in days, not months

No servers, no installation, no training manuals.

We set up your workshop

We load your customers, vehicles, old service history and parts price list from your existing Excel files, and give you your own address like yourname.jobcardbook.com.

Your team uses it on their phones

The service desk opens cards, adds parts and labour, and closes jobs. It feels like an app and can be added to the home screen.

You see everything

Reminders go out on time, reports are ready at month end, and you can check any card from anywhere. Billing stays in your existing accounting software.
